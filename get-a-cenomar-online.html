Get A CENOMAR Online

What is a CENOMAR?

A CENOMAR, or Certificate of No Marriage Record, is an official document issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) that certifies an individual has not contracted any marriage in the Philippines. This document is often required for various legal transactions, such as applying for a passport, obtaining a visa, or processing other government documents. It is also commonly used in situations where proof of single status is necessary.

Why Do You Need a CENOMAR?

There are several reasons why you might need to obtain a CENOMAR. Some of the most common include:

  • Marriage Application: When applying for a marriage license, both parties are often required to present a CENOMAR to prove they are eligible for marriage.
  • Visa Applications: Some countries require a CENOMAR as part of the visa application process to ensure the applicant is not currently married.
  • Legal Transactions: Certain legal transactions, such as property transfers or adoption processes, may require a CENOMAR.
  • Employment Requirements: Some employers, particularly those in government or sensitive positions, may require a CENOMAR as part of their background check process.

How to Get a CENOMAR Online

Thanks to technological advancements, you can now apply for a CENOMAR online, making the process more convenient and less time-consuming. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you through the process:

Step 1: Visit the PSA Serbilis Website

Go to the official PSA Serbilis website (https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph) and create an account if you do not already have one. This account will allow you to track your application and receive updates.

Step 2: Fill Out the Application Form

Once you have logged in, you will need to fill out the online application form. Be sure to provide accurate information, as any discrepancies can delay the processing of your CENOMAR. You will need to provide the following details:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Place of birth
  • Parents’ names
  • Contact information

Step 3: Choose Your Delivery Method

After completing the application form, you will be asked to choose your preferred delivery method for the CENOMAR. You can opt for:

  • Standard Delivery: The document will be delivered to your chosen address via courier.
  • Pickup: You can pick up the document at a designated PSA outlet or authorized third-party outlet.

Step 4: Pay the Processing Fee

The next step is to pay the required processing fee. The cost of a CENOMAR is typically around ₱330, but this may vary depending on the delivery method and any additional services you choose. Payment can be made through various online methods, such as credit cards, debit cards, or GCash.

Step 5: Submit Your Application

After completing the payment, review your application to ensure all information is correct. Once you are satisfied, submit your application. You will receive a confirmation email with a reference number, which you can use to track the status of your application.

Step 6: Wait for Processing and Delivery

Processing times for CENOMAR applications can vary, but it typically takes around 3 to 5 working days. You can track the status of your application using the reference number provided in your confirmation email. Once your CENOMAR is ready, it will be delivered to your chosen address or made available for pickup, depending on the option you selected.

Step 7: Verify Your CENOMAR

Upon receiving your CENOMAR, verify its authenticity by checking the security features and ensuring all information is correct. If you notice any discrepancies, contact the PSA immediately for assistance.

Conclusion

Obtaining a CENOMAR online is a straightforward process that can save you time and effort. By following the steps outlined above, you can easily apply for and receive your CENOMAR without the need to visit a physical office. Remember to keep your reference number handy and track your application to ensure a smooth and timely delivery.
