How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

The Life Path number is a numerology calculation based on your date of birth. It is usually described as a symbolic summary of the themes, motives, and tendencies associated with your path. To calculate it, you do not need a birth time or a special app: you only need your full birth date and a consistent reduction method.

For more on this, see how to calculate life path number.

What information you need

  • Your complete birth date: the day, month, and four-digit year.
  • A clear format: write the date as DDMMYYYY, such as 17031990, so no digit is omitted.
  • Pen and paper or a calculator: useful for checking each addition, although the arithmetic is simple.

Step-by-step calculation

  • Step 1: Break the date into three parts. Separate the day, month, and year. For example, 17 March 1990 becomes 17, 03, and 1990.
  • Step 2: Reduce the month. Add the digits in the month until one digit remains. For 03, that is 0 + 3 = 3.
  • Step 3: Reduce the day. Do the same with the day. For 17, 1 + 7 = 8.
  • Step 4: Reduce the year. Add all four digits and continue reducing if needed. For 1990, 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 1 + 9 = 10, then 1 + 0 = 1.
