Understanding Satta Matka: Single, Jodi, and Patti Bet Types

Satta Matka, a popular form of gambling in India, has been around for decades. It originated in the 1950s and gained significant traction in the 1960s and 1970s. The game involves betting on numbers, and there are various ways to place bets. Among the most common bet types in Satta Matka are Single, Jodi, and Patti. This article aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of these bet types to help both beginners and experienced players make informed decisions.

What is Satta Matka?

Satta Matka is a number-based lottery game that originated in Mumbai. It involves drawing random numbers to determine the winner. The game has evolved over the years, and today, it is played both offline and online. The basic premise remains the same: players bet on numbers, and if their chosen numbers are drawn, they win a prize.

Types of Satta Matka Bets

There are several ways to place bets in Satta Matka, but the most common ones are Single, Jodi, and Patti. Each of these bet types has its own rules and potential payouts.

1. Single

A Single bet is one of the simplest forms of betting in Satta Matka. In this type of bet, players choose a single digit from 0 to 9. The result of the game is determined by the draw of three numbers. If the chosen single digit matches any of the three numbers drawn, the player wins.

How to Play:

  • Choose a single digit between 0 and 9.
  • Place your bet on that number.
  • Wait for the draw to see if your number is selected.

Payout: The payout for a Single bet can vary depending on the bookie and the odds set. Typically, the payout is higher due to the lower probability of winning.

2. Jodi

A Jodi bet involves betting on a pair of numbers. In this type of bet, players choose a pair of digits, each ranging from 00 to 99. The result of the game is determined by the draw of two numbers, and if the chosen pair matches the drawn pair, the player wins.

How to Play:

  • Choose a pair of numbers between 00 and 99.
  • Place your bet on that pair.
  • Wait for the draw to see if your pair is selected.

Payout: The payout for a Jodi bet is generally lower than that of a Single bet because the probability of winning is higher. However, the exact payout can vary based on the bookie and the odds.

3. Patti

A Patti bet is a bit more complex than Single and Jodi bets. In this type of bet, players choose a three-digit number, and the result is determined by the draw of three numbers. The Patti is the last digit of the sum of the three drawn numbers. If the chosen Patti matches the last digit of the sum, the player wins.

How to Play:

  • Choose a three-digit number.
  • Place your bet on that number.
  • Wait for the draw to see if the last digit of the sum of the three drawn numbers matches your chosen Patti.

Payout: The payout for a Patti bet can be quite high due to the lower probability of winning. The exact payout will depend on the bookie and the odds set for the game.
