Dishwasher Not Turning On

Dishwasher not turning on: begin with a safe, systematic check

A dishwasher that will not turn on may have a simple control issue, a loss of power, or a failed door latch. The exact symptom matters. Note whether there are no lights or sounds, the display works but a cycle will not start, or the machine starts and then stops. This distinction helps you avoid unnecessary repairs.

  • Locate the model information. Find the manual or model number before changing settings. Control symbols and reset procedures vary between manufacturers.
  • Keep the area dry and powered down safely. If water is near the plug or outlet, switch off the circuit breaker before touching anything electrical.
  • Avoid disassembly. Do not remove the kickplate, control panel, or covers while the dishwasher is connected to power.

1. Confirm that power is reaching the dishwasher

First determine whether the control panel is completely dead. Some models display a clock even when they are not ready to run, so a lit display does not necessarily mean every control is working.

  • Check the circuit breaker. Look for a tripped or switched-off breaker serving the kitchen. If a dishwasher-specific breaker exists, check it separately.
  • Check the plug or outlet. Unplug the dishwasher safely, inspect the plug for damage, and confirm that the receptacle has power. A known-working lamp or plug-in outlet tester can help identify a dead outlet.
  • Check for a secondary power control. Some installations include a disconnect switch, while certain models have a delay-start or power-control feature that must be activated.
  • Look for visible damage. A loose plug, scorched outlet, damaged cord, or repeated breaker trip requires attention before operating the appliance again.

2. Verify the controls and selected settings

If the panel lights or responds when a button is pressed, the dishwasher is receiving power but may be locked or waiting for a specific setting.

  • Press the power button on models that have one, then select a wash cycle and press Start.
  • Check for a delay-start indicator. Cancel or clear the delay according to the manual.
  • Look for a Control Lock or Child Lock message. Unlock it using the model-specific button or combination.
  • Make sure the display is not showing an error code. Record the code before resetting or servicing the dishwasher.
  • Avoid repeatedly pressing several buttons at once, which can place some models into a diagnostic mode rather than starting a cycle.

3. Check the door latch and basic connections

A dishwasher normally will not begin unless its door is fully closed and the latch engages. If the panel responds but the machine never starts, inspect the latch before assuming the motor or control board has failed.

  • Close the door firmly until you hear a click. Do not force it if it catches or remains uneven.
  • Check that no dish, rack, or utensil blocks the door.
  • On a new installation, remove any shipping bracket that may be preventing the door from closing completely.
  • If the machine powers up but never fills, confirm that the under-sink water valve is open and that the inlet hose is connected.

4. Try a controlled power reset

A temporary control problem may clear when the dishwasher is fully disconnected. Turn off the appropriate breaker, unplug the unit if it is safely accessible, and wait several minutes before restoring power.

  • After reconnecting power, select a normal cycle and press Start.
  • If the manual provides a model-specific reset procedure, follow it rather than guessing which buttons to hold.
  • If the breaker trips again during this test, stop troubleshooting and arrange service.

5. Know when professional service is needed

Some faults are not suitable for user troubleshooting. Switch off the breaker and contact a qualified appliance technician if you notice burning odors, buzzing, sparks, damaged wiring, water near electrical components, or repeated breaker trips. Also seek help when an error code remains after a reset, the door latch appears damaged, or the dishwasher was recently installed and still has no power. If the appliance is under warranty, contact the manufacturer before attempting disassembly.

6. Reduce future problems

Routine care can prevent common operating issues:

  • Clean the filter and accessible spray-arm openings as directed.
  • Avoid overloading the racks or blocking the door from closing.
  • Secure inlet and drain hoses and check them periodically for leaks or kinks.
