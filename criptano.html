What is Criptano?

Criptano is a portmanteau of "criptografía" (cryptography) and "humano" (human). It refers to the integration of cryptographic technologies with human-centered design principles to create more secure, efficient, and user-friendly digital systems. Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that focus primarily on decentralization and security, Criptano emphasizes the human experience, making it more accessible to the average user.

The concept of Criptano emerged as a response to the growing complexity of blockchain technologies and the need for more intuitive interfaces. It seeks to bridge the gap between the technical aspects of cryptography and the practical needs of everyday users.
