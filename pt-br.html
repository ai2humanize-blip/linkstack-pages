Introduction to PT BR

The Portuguese language was brought to Brazil in the early 16th century by Portuguese explorers and settlers. Over the centuries, the language underwent significant changes due to the influence of indigenous languages, African languages brought by enslaved people, and later, other European languages due to immigration.

  • African Influence: The African influence is evident in words and cultural expressions, especially in regions with a strong Afro-Brazilian presence. Words like "samba" and "quilombo" have African roots.
  • European Immigration: In the late 19th and early 20th centuries, large numbers of immigrants from Italy, Germany, Spain, and other European countries arrived in Brazil, contributing to the linguistic diversity and influencing the evolution of PT BR.
