Serpins, short for serine protease inhibitors, are a superfamily of proteins found in all domains of life, from viruses to humans. They are primarily known for their role in regulating proteases, which are enzymes that break down proteins. By inhibiting these proteases, serpins help maintain the balance of protein levels in the body, which is essential for normal cellular function.
