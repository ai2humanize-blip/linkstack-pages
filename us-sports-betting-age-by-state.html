This article aims to provide a clear overview of the sports betting age requirements by state, helping you navigate the legal landscape and ensure you bet responsibly and legally.

Here is a breakdown of the general guidelines:

  • 18 Years Old: Some states allow individuals who are 18 and older to participate in sports betting. However, this is less common and typically applies to states where other forms of gambling, such as lottery and horse racing, are also accessible at this age.
  • 21 Years Old: The majority of states with legal sports betting require bettors to be at least 21 years old. This aligns with the legal drinking age and is the standard for casino gambling in most states.
