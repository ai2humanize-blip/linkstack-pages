Understanding US Sports Betting Age by State

Sports betting has seen a significant transformation in the United States over recent years. With the repeal of the Professional and Amateur Sports Protection Act (PASPA) in 2018, individual states have been given the autonomy to legalize and regulate sports betting within their borders. However, one of the most crucial aspects of sports betting regulation is the age requirement, which varies from state to state. This article provides a comprehensive overview of the sports betting age by state, helping you understand the legal landscape.

General Overview of Sports Betting Age in the US

In the United States, the legal age for gambling, including sports betting, is primarily determined by each state. While most states set the minimum age at 21, some allow individuals as young as 18 to participate in certain types of gambling. The variation in age requirements is due to the different gambling laws and the types of betting activities allowed in each state.

Here are some key points to consider:

  • Federal vs. State Laws: Although there is no federal law that mandates a specific age for sports betting, states have the authority to set their own regulations.
  • Types of Gambling: The age requirement can also depend on the type of gambling activity. For instance, lottery and horse racing may have different age restrictions compared to sports betting.
  • Online vs. In-Person Betting: Some states may have different age requirements for online sports betting compared to in-person betting at physical locations.

States Where the Legal Sports Betting Age is 21

The majority of states that have legalized sports betting set the legal age at 21. This is consistent with the legal drinking age in the US, which may contribute to the decision to set the sports betting age at 21. Here are some of the states that fall into this category:

  • Nevada: Known for its vibrant gambling scene, Nevada has allowed sports betting since 1949. The legal age for sports betting in Nevada is 21.
  • New Jersey: Following the repeal of PASPA, New Jersey quickly legalized sports betting. The legal age for both in-person and online sports betting is 21.
  • Pennsylvania: Pennsylvania allows sports betting at casinos and online, with a minimum age of 21.
  • Illinois: In Illinois, the legal age for sports betting is 21, whether betting in-person or online.
  • Colorado: Colorado's sports betting law also sets the age at 21.

States Where the Legal Sports Betting Age is 18

While most states require bettors to be at least 21, some states allow individuals aged 18 and older to participate in sports betting. These states typically have different regulations and types of betting activities that influence the age requirement. Some of the states with an 18-year-old minimum age include:

  • Wyoming: Wyoming legalized online sports betting in 2021, setting the age limit at 18.
  • New Hampshire: In New Hampshire, the legal age for sports betting is 18, but this is primarily for online betting through the state lottery.
  • Montana: Montana allows sports betting through the state lottery, with an age limit of 18.
  • Rhode Island: Rhode Island permits sports betting at age 18, but this is limited to in-person betting at designated locations.

Additional Considerations

It's important to note that while the legal age for sports betting is determined by state laws, individual sportsbooks and casinos may have their own policies that require patrons to be 21 or older. Additionally, some states may have specific restrictions or exceptions based on the type of sports betting or the venue where the betting takes place.
