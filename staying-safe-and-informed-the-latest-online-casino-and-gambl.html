One of the most significant technological advancements in online gambling is the use of blockchain technology. This technology is being adopted by many online casinos to enhance security and fairness.

Another innovation is the use of artificial intelligence (AI) to detect and prevent problem gambling. AI algorithms can analyze player behavior and identify patterns that may indicate compulsive gambling. This allows online casinos to intervene and offer support to players who may be at risk.
