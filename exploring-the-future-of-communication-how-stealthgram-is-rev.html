The Rise of Privacy-Focused Communication Apps

Key Features of Privacy-Focused Apps

  • No Data Storage: Minimal data retention policies that limit the amount of user data stored on servers.
  • Open-Source Code: Allows for public scrutiny and transparency in the app's security measures.
