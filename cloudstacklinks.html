What is CloudStack?

Before diving into CloudStackLinks, it is essential to understand what CloudStack is. Apache CloudStack is an open-source software designed to deploy and manage large networks of virtual machines, as a highly available, highly scalable Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) cloud computing platform. It is used by a number of service providers to offer public cloud services, and by enterprises to provide an on-premises (private) cloud offering or as part of a hybrid cloud solution.
