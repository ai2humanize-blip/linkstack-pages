Understanding Tokenized Carry Funds

A tokenized carry fund is a type of investment vehicle that leverages blockchain technology to tokenize the carry strategy, which involves earning returns from the difference in interest rates between two currencies or assets. These funds are designed to provide investors with exposure to carry trade strategies in a more accessible, transparent, and liquid manner compared to traditional methods.

Tokenization allows for the fractional ownership of assets, enabling investors to buy and sell tokens representing their share of the fund. This democratizes access to sophisticated investment strategies that were previously available only to large institutional players.
