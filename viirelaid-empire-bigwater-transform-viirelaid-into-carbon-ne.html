Understanding Carbon Negativity

Carbon negativity refers to the state where an entity removes more carbon dioxide from the atmosphere than it emits. This is a step beyond carbon neutrality, which involves balancing carbon emissions with carbon removal or offsetting. Achieving carbon negativity requires a comprehensive strategy that includes reducing emissions, enhancing carbon sinks, and investing in innovative technologies.
