Understanding Carbon Negativity

Carbon negativity refers to the state where an entity removes more carbon dioxide from the atmosphere than it emits. This is a step beyond carbon neutrality, which only balances emissions with removal. Achieving carbon negativity is crucial in the fight against climate change, as it helps to actively reduce the overall concentration of CO2 in the atmosphere.
