Understanding Carbon Negativity

Carbon negativity goes beyond achieving net-zero carbon emissions. It involves removing more carbon dioxide from the atmosphere than is emitted. This ambitious goal requires a multifaceted approach that includes reducing emissions, enhancing natural carbon sinks, and employing advanced technologies for carbon capture and storage.

For Viirelaid, becoming carbon negative would not only benefit the local environment but also set a global example for sustainable living and community-driven environmental action.
