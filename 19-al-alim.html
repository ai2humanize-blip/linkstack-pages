What is "19 Al Alim"?

The term "19 Al Alim" originates from the Arabic language, where "19" refers to the number and "Al Alim" translates to "The All-Knowing." In Islamic theology, "Al Alim" is one of the 99 names of Allah, signifying God's attribute of omniscience. The number 19, on the other hand, holds a unique place in Islamic numerology and scripture.

The number 19 is significant in the Quran, as mentioned in Surah Al-Muddaththir (Chapter 74), verse 30: "Over it are nineteen." This verse has been the subject of much scholarly interpretation, with some suggesting that it refers to the number of angels guarding Hell, while others propose more symbolic meanings.
