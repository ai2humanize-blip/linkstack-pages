What is XRP and Ripple?

XRP is the native digital asset of the Ripple network, a real-time gross settlement system, currency exchange, and remittance network. Ripple Labs, the company behind XRP, aims to facilitate faster, cheaper, and more reliable global transactions compared to traditional financial systems. While XRP is often referred to as "Ripple" due to its association with the company, it is important to distinguish between the two:

  • XRP: The digital asset used for transactions on the Ripple network.
  • Ripple: The company that developed the Ripple protocol and network.

Understanding this distinction is crucial for grasping the dynamics of XRP's price and its market behavior.
