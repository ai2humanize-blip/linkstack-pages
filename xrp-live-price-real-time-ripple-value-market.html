What is XRP?

XRP is a digital currency that was created by Ripple Labs, a technology company focused on providing fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, which rely on a proof-of-work consensus mechanism, XRP uses a unique consensus protocol called the XRP Ledger Consensus Protocol. This allows for faster transaction times and lower fees, making it an attractive option for financial institutions and individuals seeking efficient cross-border payments.

The primary purpose of XRP is to act as a bridge currency in the Ripple network, facilitating the exchange of different currencies quickly and cost-effectively. This unique use case has positioned XRP as a key player in the global remittance market.
