What is XRP and Ripple?

XRP is the native digital asset of the Ripple network, a real-time gross settlement system, currency exchange, and remittance network. Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, which rely on blockchain mining, XRP operates on a consensus protocol managed by a network of validating servers. Ripple's technology aims to facilitate fast, low-cost international money transfers and payments.

Ripple, the company behind XRP, has positioned itself as a solution for financial institutions seeking to improve cross-border transaction efficiency. By using XRP, these institutions can benefit from faster transaction times and lower costs compared to traditional methods.
