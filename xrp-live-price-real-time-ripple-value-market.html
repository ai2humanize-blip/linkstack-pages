What is XRP?

XRP is a digital asset built for payments. It is the native cryptocurrency of the XRP Ledger, an open-source, decentralized blockchain technology developed by Ripple Labs. Unlike Bitcoin, which was designed primarily as a digital currency, XRP aims to facilitate faster and more cost-effective cross-border transactions for financial institutions.

One of the key features of XRP is its ability to settle transactions in just a few seconds, making it an attractive option for banks and payment providers looking to improve their transaction processing times.
