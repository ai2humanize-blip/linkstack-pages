What is XRP?

XRP is the native cryptocurrency of the Ripple network, a digital payment protocol designed to facilitate fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, which are often used for peer-to-peer transactions, XRP is primarily used by financial institutions and payment providers to enable cross-border payments. The Ripple network's underlying technology, known as Ripple Protocol Consensus Algorithm (RPCA), allows for swift transaction confirmations, making it an attractive option for banks and other financial entities.
