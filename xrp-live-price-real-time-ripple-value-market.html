What is XRP?

XRP is the native cryptocurrency of the Ripple network, a digital payment protocol designed to facilitate fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin, which was designed as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system, Ripple focuses on providing solutions for banks and financial institutions to improve cross-border transactions.

XRP serves as a bridge currency in the Ripple network, allowing for the seamless exchange of different fiat currencies. This unique utility has positioned XRP as a significant player in the cryptocurrency market.
