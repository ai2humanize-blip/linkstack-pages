What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a technical chart pattern that is commonly associated with bullish reversals in the market. It is characterized by a contracting price range that slopes downward, with the lower trendline having a steeper decline than the upper trendline. This pattern typically forms over a period of several weeks to several months and is often seen as a sign of consolidation before a potential breakout.

  • Characteristics of a Falling Wedge:
    • Both trendlines are sloping downwards.
    • The upper trendline acts as a resistance level.
    • The lower trendline acts as a support level.
    • Volume typically decreases as the pattern progresses.
    • A breakout occurs when the price breaks above the upper trendline.
  • Significance of the Pattern:
    • It suggests a period of consolidation where sellers lose momentum.
    • It often precedes a bullish reversal, indicating a potential upward price movement.
    • The breakout is usually accompanied by an increase in trading volume.
