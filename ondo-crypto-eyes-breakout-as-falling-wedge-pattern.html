What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a bullish chart pattern that indicates a potential reversal in the current downtrend. It is characterized by a series of lower highs and lower lows, which converge to form a triangular shape. Unlike a descending triangle, which has a horizontal support line, both the support and resistance lines of a falling wedge slope downward.

The falling wedge pattern is considered a bullish signal because it suggests that the downward momentum is slowing down, and the asset is preparing for a potential upward breakout. Traders often look for this pattern as an opportunity to enter a long position or to add to their existing positions.

Identifying a Falling Wedge Pattern

To identify a falling wedge pattern, traders should look for the following characteristics:

  • Downward Sloping Trend Lines: Both the resistance and support lines should be sloping downwards, indicating a decrease in both the highs and lows.
  • Convergence of Trend Lines: The distance between the highs and lows should be narrowing, forming a triangular shape.
  • Volume: As the pattern progresses, trading volume typically decreases, indicating a consolidation phase. A surge in volume during the breakout is a strong confirmation of the pattern.
  • Breakout: The breakout occurs when the price breaks above the upper trend line (resistance). This is often accompanied by an increase in trading volume.

When these elements are present, it suggests that the asset is likely to experience a bullish reversal.
