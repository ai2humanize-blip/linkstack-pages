What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a bullish chart pattern that indicates a potential reversal in the current downtrend. This pattern is characterized by a narrowing price range as the asset's price moves lower, creating a wedge shape on the chart. The key features of a falling wedge include:

  • Converging Trend Lines: The pattern is formed by two trend lines—one descending resistance line and one descending support line—that converge as the pattern matures.
  • Declining Volume: As the wedge forms, trading volume typically decreases, indicating a potential pause in the selling pressure.
  • Breakout Point: The breakout occurs when the price breaches the upper resistance line, often accompanied by a surge in volume.

Traders interpret the falling wedge as a bullish signal because it suggests that the downward momentum is weakening, and a reversal might be imminent.
