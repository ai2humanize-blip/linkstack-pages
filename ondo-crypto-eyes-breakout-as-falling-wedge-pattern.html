What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a technical chart pattern that indicates a potential reversal in the current trend. It is characterized by a contracting price range, where both the support and resistance lines are sloping downward but converging towards each other. This pattern is often considered bullish, as it suggests that the downward momentum is slowing down and a breakout to the upside may be imminent.

Here are some key characteristics of a falling wedge pattern:

  • Downward-sloping trend lines: Both the resistance and support lines are angled downwards.
  • Converging trend lines: The distance between the support and resistance lines narrows over time.
  • Decreasing volume: Trading volume typically decreases as the pattern progresses, indicating a slowdown in selling pressure.
  • Breakout: A breakout occurs when the price breaches the upper trend line, often accompanied by a surge in volume.
