What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a bullish chart pattern that is often seen in technical analysis. It is characterized by a series of lower highs and lower lows, which form two converging trend lines. The pattern suggests a period of consolidation where the price is being squeezed between the two trend lines, indicating a potential breakout to the upside.

  • Lower Highs and Lower Lows: The pattern is formed by a series of lower highs and lower lows, creating a downward-sloping channel.
  • Converging Trend Lines: The upper and lower trend lines converge, indicating a decrease in price volatility.
  • Bullish Signal: The falling wedge is typically considered a bullish signal, suggesting a potential reversal and breakout to the upside.

Traders often look for a breakout above the upper trend line to confirm the pattern and anticipate a potential price increase. The breakout is usually accompanied by a surge in trading volume, adding to the validity of the signal.
