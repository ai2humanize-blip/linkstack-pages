What is a Falling Wedge Pattern?

A falling wedge is a technical analysis pattern that suggests a potential bullish reversal in the price of an asset. It is characterized by a downward price movement within two converging trend lines. Here are the key features of a falling wedge:

  • Converging Trend Lines: The pattern is formed by two trend lines that converge as the price moves lower. The upper trend line, or the resistance line, has a steeper decline than the lower trend line, or the support line.
  • Decreasing Volume: As the pattern develops, trading volume typically decreases, indicating a weakening of the selling pressure.
  • Breakout: A breakout occurs when the price breaches the upper trend line, signaling a potential reversal and the beginning of an upward trend.

The falling wedge is considered a bullish pattern because it suggests that the downward trend is losing momentum and that a reversal is likely.
