Names That Mean "Gift": A Beautiful Choice for Your Child

Names for Girls That Mean "Gift"

When it comes to girls' names that mean "gift," there are several lovely options to choose from. These names not only sound beautiful but also carry a profound meaning.

  • Dorothy: This classic name has Greek origins and means "gift of God." It has been a popular choice for centuries and continues to be cherished for its timeless appeal.
  • Jessica: With Hebrew origins, Jessica means "God's gift." It gained popularity after Shakespeare used it in his play "The Merchant of Venice" and has remained a favorite ever since.
  • Gratia: This Latin name directly translates to "gift" or "grace." It is a unique and elegant choice for parents seeking a less common name.
  • Mahal: Of Arabic origin, Mahal means "gift" or "favor." It is a simple yet meaningful name that is not overly common, making it a distinctive choice.
  • Theresa: This name has Greek roots and means "harvester" or "gift from God." It has been a popular name in many Western countries and is associated with kindness and compassion.

Names for Boys That Mean "Gift"

For boys, there are also plenty of names that convey the idea of a gift. These names are strong, meaningful, and carry a sense of purpose.

  • Jonathan: With Hebrew origins, Jonathan means "gift of God." It is a classic name that has been popular for generations and is associated with loyalty and friendship.
  • Matthew: Also of Hebrew origin, Matthew means "gift of God." It is a well-loved name with a rich history and is often associated with intelligence and leadership.
  • Nathan: This name means "gift" in Hebrew. It is a popular choice for boys and is known for its simplicity and strength.
  • Dayan: With Hebrew origins, Dayan means "gift from God." It is a less common name, offering a unique option for parents seeking something distinctive.
  • Ezekiel: This name means "God will strengthen" or "God's gift" in Hebrew. It is a powerful name with a rich biblical history and is often associated with wisdom and insight.

Gender-Neutral Names That Mean "Gift"

If you are looking for a gender-neutral name that means "gift," there are several options that work well for any child, regardless of gender.

  • Jesse: This name has Hebrew origins and means "gift." It is a versatile name that can be used for both boys and girls, offering a modern and fresh appeal.

  • Zenon: This Greek name means "gift from Zeus." It is a strong and distinctive name that can be used for both boys and girls.
  • Amara: Although often used for girls, Amara is a gender-neutral name of African origin that means "grace" or "gift." It is a beautiful and meaningful choice for any child.

Conclusion

Choosing a name that means "gift" is a wonderful way to honor the blessing that your child is to you. Whether you prefer a classic, modern, or unique name, there are plenty of options to consider.
