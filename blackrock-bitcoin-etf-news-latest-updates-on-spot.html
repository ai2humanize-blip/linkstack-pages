What is a Bitcoin ETF?

A Bitcoin ETF is a type of investment fund that tracks the price of Bitcoin and is traded on traditional stock exchanges. Unlike directly buying and holding Bitcoin, an ETF allows investors to gain exposure to Bitcoin without the need to manage the cryptocurrency themselves. This includes handling private keys, navigating cryptocurrency exchanges, and dealing with security concerns.

ETFs are popular because they offer a more familiar and regulated environment for traditional investors. They also provide liquidity, transparency, and the ability to buy and sell shares throughout the trading day.

BlackRock's Foray into Bitcoin ETF

In June 2023, BlackRock, with over $9 trillion in assets under management, filed an application with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to launch a spot Bitcoin ETF. This move marked a significant shift in the landscape, as BlackRock's entry into the cryptocurrency space is seen as a major endorsement of Bitcoin's potential as an asset class.

The proposed ETF, named the iShares Bitcoin Trust, aims to provide investors with a secure and cost-effective way to gain exposure to Bitcoin. BlackRock's application has sparked a wave of interest and anticipation among investors and industry stakeholders.
