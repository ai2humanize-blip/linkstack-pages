How to Tell Explainer Journalism from Opinion — a quick guide

Most of us read online every day, yet few of us stop to think about how to do it well.

  • Subjective Perspective: The content is inherently biased, as it reflects the author's personal beliefs, interpretations, and judgments.
  • Argumentative Nature: Opinion pieces frequently involve arguments, critiques, or advocacy for a particular position or course of action.
  • Engagement and Discussion: The aim is to engage readers in a dialogue, encouraging them to think critically about the issue at hand and possibly form their own opinions.
  • Editorial Judgment: The inclusion of the author's editorial judgment is a hallmark, as they weigh evidence and present a case for their viewpoint.
