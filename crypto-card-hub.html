Crypto Card Hub

What Is a Crypto Card Hub?

A crypto card hub is a centralized platform or directory that helps people compare, manage, or access crypto-enabled payment cards. It may connect a card to a cryptocurrency exchange, a custodial account, or a self-custody wallet. Some hubs are primarily comparison services; others provide an interface for managing or issuing cards. The label alone does not tell you who holds your money or which regulations apply, so confirm the legal entity, card issuer, and custody model before using one.

How Crypto Cards Work

Crypto cards most commonly operate as prepaid or debit cards. When a purchase is made, the provider may convert the card balance into local currency and settle the charge through its payment network. Features vary by service and location, and may include ATM access, refunds, merchant controls, spending limits, and rewards. A hub can make these features easier to compare, but the card issuer remains responsible for issuing the card and processing transactions.
