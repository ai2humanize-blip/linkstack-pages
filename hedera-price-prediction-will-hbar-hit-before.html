Introduction to Hedera Hashgraph and HBAR

Hedera Hashgraph is a distributed ledger technology that offers a platform for developing decentralized applications (dApps). Unlike traditional blockchain technologies, Hedera uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG) to achieve consensus, which promises faster transaction speeds and greater security. The native cryptocurrency of the Hedera network is HBAR, which is used for transaction fees, staking, and network security.
