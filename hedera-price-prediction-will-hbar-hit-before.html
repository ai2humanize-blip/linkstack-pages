What is Hedera (HBAR)?

Hedera Hashgraph is a distributed ledger technology that aims to offer a more efficient and secure alternative to traditional blockchain systems. Unlike conventional blockchains, Hedera uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG) structure, which allows for high transaction speeds and low fees. The platform is designed to support a wide range of applications, including decentralized finance (DeFi), supply chain management, and more.

HBAR is the native cryptocurrency of the Hedera network, used for securing the network through staking, paying for transaction fees, and as a means of value transfer.
