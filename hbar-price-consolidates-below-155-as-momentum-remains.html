Introduction to Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR)

Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR) is a cryptocurrency that operates on the Hedera public network, which is known for its speed, security, and fairness. Unlike traditional blockchain technology, Hedera uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG) to achieve consensus, making it a unique player in the crypto space. The HBAR token serves multiple purposes within the network, including securing the network through staking and enabling transactions and smart contracts.
