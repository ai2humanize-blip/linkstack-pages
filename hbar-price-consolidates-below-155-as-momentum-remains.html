What is Price Consolidation?

Price consolidation refers to a period where an asset's price moves within a narrow range, neither breaking out to new highs nor falling to new lows. This phase is often characterized by low trading volumes and reduced volatility. For hbar, consolidation below $0.155 suggests that the market is taking a pause, potentially digesting previous gains or waiting for new catalysts to drive the price.
