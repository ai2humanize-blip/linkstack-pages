What is Price Consolidation?

Price consolidation refers to a period where an asset's price trades within a relatively tight range after a significant move up or down. This phase is often characterized by low trading volumes and a decrease in volatility. For HBAR, the consolidation below $0.155 indicates that the market is taking a pause after a period of activity, allowing traders and investors to reassess their positions.

During consolidation, the asset's price tends to bounce between a specific high and low point, creating a range. For HBAR, this range is currently below the $0.155 resistance level. This behavior suggests that while there is some selling pressure at higher prices, there is also enough buying interest to prevent the price from dropping significantly.
