What is Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR)?

Hedera Hashgraph is a decentralized public network that offers a new form of distributed consensus. Unlike traditional blockchain technology, Hedera uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG) to achieve high transaction speeds and security. The network's native cryptocurrency, HBAR, is used to power the platform, facilitate transactions, and provide security through staking.

HBAR has gained attention for its potential to support a wide range of applications, from decentralized finance (DeFi) to supply chain management and more. Its unique consensus mechanism allows for high throughput and low fees, making it an attractive option for developers and enterprises.
