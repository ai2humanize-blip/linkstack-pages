What Are Crypto Cards?

Crypto cards are payment cards that enable users to spend their cryptocurrency holdings at any merchant that accepts card payments. These cards essentially convert your digital currency into fiat money at the point of sale, allowing for smooth and efficient transactions. There are two main types of crypto cards:

  • Debit Cards: These cards are linked directly to your cryptocurrency wallet. When you make a purchase, the equivalent value of cryptocurrency is debited from your wallet and converted into fiat currency.
  • Prepaid Cards: These cards require you to load a specific amount of cryptocurrency onto the card, which is then converted into fiat currency. You can use the card until the loaded balance is depleted.
