How to Install Ivanaturf

Installing Ivanaturf is a straightforward process, but it requires careful planning and execution to ensure the best results. Here are the basic steps:

  • Site Preparation: Begin by clearing the area of any debris, rocks, or weeds. Till the soil to a depth of about 4-6 inches to ensure good root penetration.
  • Soil Testing and Amendment: Conduct a soil test to determine if any nutrients are lacking. Amend the soil with compost or other organic matter to improve its fertility and drainage.
  • Laying the Turf: Lay the Ivanaturf rolls or squares tightly together, ensuring that the edges are snug. Stagger the joints in a brick-like pattern to prevent gaps.
  • Watering: Immediately after installation, water the turf thoroughly. Continue to water daily for the first few weeks to help the roots establish themselves.
  • First Mowing: Once the grass reaches a height of about 3 inches, it is ready for its first mowing. Be sure to use a sharp blade to avoid tearing the grass.

Maintenance Tips for Ivanaturf

To keep your Ivanaturf looking its best, follow these maintenance tips:

  • Regular Watering: While Ivanaturf is relatively drought-resistant, it still requires regular watering, especially during dry spells. Water deeply and infrequently to encourage deep root growth.
  • Fertilization: Apply a balanced fertilizer in the spring and fall to provide the necessary nutrients for healthy growth. Be careful not to over-fertilize, as this can lead to excessive growth and increased maintenance.
  • Mowing: Mow regularly, but avoid cutting more than one-third of the grass height at a time. This helps to maintain a healthy lawn and prevents stress on the grass.
  • Weed Control: Use pre-emergent and post-emergent herbicides as needed to control weeds. Regular mowing and proper fertilization can also help to minimize weed growth.
