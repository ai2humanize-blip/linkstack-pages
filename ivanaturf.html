Installation and Maintenance

Installing Ivanaturf is a straightforward process, but it is recommended to hire a professional to ensure the best results. Here are some general steps involved in the installation:

  • Site Preparation: The area where the turf will be installed must be cleared of debris and leveled properly.
  • Base Installation: A base layer of crushed stone or other materials is laid down to provide stability and drainage.
  • Turf Installation: The Ivanaturf is rolled out and cut to fit the area. It is then secured in place with nails or other fasteners.
  • Infill Application: Infill material, such as sand or rubber, is applied to the turf to add weight and stability and to help the fibers stand upright.

Once installed, Ivanaturf requires minimal maintenance. Regular brushing to keep the fibers upright and occasional cleaning to remove debris are usually sufficient. For pet owners, rinsing the area with water can help maintain cleanliness.
