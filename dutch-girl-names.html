  • Floor: A short and sweet name that means "flower." Floor is a popular name in the Netherlands and has a charming, simple appeal.
  • Linda: Meaning "beautiful" or "pretty," Linda was extremely popular in the Netherlands in the mid-20th century and remains a classic choice.
  • Emma: A name that means "whole" or "universal," Emma has been a popular choice in the Netherlands and many other countries.
  • Anneke: A diminutive of Anna, Anneke is a sweet and charming name that means "grace." It is a popular choice for parents looking for a more informal version of Anna.
  • Noor: Meaning "light," Noor is a beautiful and unique name that has been gaining popularity in the Netherlands and beyond.
