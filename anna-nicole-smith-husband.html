Her First Husband: Michael Smith

Smith married Michael Smith, a tattoo artist, in Mexico in 1993. The marriage was extremely brief and was annulled in 1994. Their relationship is often described as youthful and short-lived, reflecting the fact that both were very young when they married.

Although the marriage ended quickly, it is an important part of Smith’s biography because some biographies and retrospective articles mention her first husband alongside later relationships. However, Michael Smith is not generally the person meant when someone asks about the husband Anna Nicole Smith was with near the end of her life.

Her Second Husband: Travis James

Travis James was a firefighter and licensed mortgage broker in Las Vegas. He became Anna Nicole Smith’s second and final legal husband when they married in May 2007. Their wedding took place at the Hard Rock Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas.

Their marriage lasted only a few months. James filed for divorce in September 2007, and the divorce was formally finalized in 2008. Because Smith died in February 2007, the legal dissolution of their marriage occurred after her death. For anyone searching specifically for the husband she married in her final years, Travis James is the most direct answer.

Stephen Bear Smith, a former model and musician, is frequently mentioned in connection with Anna Nicole Smith because of their long relationship and their daughter, Pamela. Despite their extensive history together, the two were never legally married.
