Anna Nicole Smith’s Husbands: Her Two Marriages Explained

Anna Nicole Smith, the American model, television personality, and singer, was legally married twice. Her first husband was Ryan Shamrock, whom she married in 1993. Her second and final husband was Theodore J. “Bobby” Smith, a Texas firearms manufacturer from the Smith & Wesson family. Because his surname matched the name Smith used throughout her career, many people who search for “Anna Nicole Smith husband” are specifically looking for information about Bobby Smith.

Her First Husband: Ryan Shamrock

Anna Nicole Smith married Ryan Shamrock in 1993. Shamrock worked as a firefighter, and the couple had a son, Charles Anthony Smith, commonly called Justin. Their marriage began to break down not long after the birth of their child.

The couple separated in 1995, but their divorce was not finalized until 1999. This distinction explains why some biographies describe Anna Nicole Smith as married to Shamrock from 1993 to 1995, while others list the marriage through 1999. The separation and the later legal proceedings were separate stages of the same divorce.

  • Husband: Ryan Shamrock
  • Marriage began: 1993
  • Separated: 1995
  • Divorce finalized: 1999
  • Child: Charles Anthony “Justin” Smith

Her Second Husband: Theodore J. “Bobby” Smith

Smith married Theodore J. “Bobby” Smith on March 4, 2006. Bobby Smith was a Texas-based firearms manufacturer and a member of the family associated with the Smith & Wesson business. Their marriage became the focus of extensive national coverage because of its timing and the sudden family tragedy that followed.

Bobby Smith died unexpectedly from a heart attack in October 2006, less than seven months after the wedding. Their infant son, Charles Anthony Smith II, died several days later following medical complications connected to his premature birth. Anna Nicole Smith died on February 8, 2007, less than four months after her husband.

  • Marriage date: March 4, 2006
  • Marriage location: Texas
  • Bobby Smith’s death: October 2006
  • Child: Charles Anthony Smith II

Why “Anna Nicole Smith Husband” Can Be Confusing

Several names and relationships are often blended together in short online biographies. Anna Nicole Smith was born Anna Nicole Malhotra, but “Smith” became her professional surname and was also the name of her second husband. Her first husband was Ryan Shamrock, while her son from that marriage was Charles Anthony Smith, known as Justin. The two husbands therefore had different surnames, even though family members and later accounts frequently referred to the Smith name.

There is also a difference between a marriage ending and a divorce being finalized. That is particularly important when researching her first marriage: 1995 refers to the separation from Ryan Shamrock, whereas 1999 refers to the completed divorce. For her second marriage, there was no long public separation or contested divorce timeline; Bobby Smith’s unexpected death ended the marriage only months after the ceremony.

Which Husband Is Most Often Meant?

If a source refers to Anna Nicole Smith’s husband in the context of her later life, media coverage, or her final years, it usually means Theodore J. “Bobby” Smith. If the reference concerns her son Justin, her early adult life, or the period before her second marriage, it is generally Ryan Shamrock.

The clearest way to understand her personal history is as two distinct chapters: her marriage to Ryan Shamrock, which produced her eldest son, and her brief marriage to Bobby Smith, which ended in profound loss. Either way, the phrase “Anna Nicole Smith husband” points to a subject shaped by two very different periods of her life.
