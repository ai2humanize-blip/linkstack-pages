What Are Bonus Buy Features?

Bonus buy features, also known as feature buys or bonus bets, are options that allow players to pay a fixed amount to access the game's bonus round immediately. This feature is particularly popular in video slots, where bonus rounds often offer the highest payouts and most engaging gameplay. By purchasing the bonus, players can bypass the requirement of landing a specific number of scatter symbols or other triggering conditions.

Bonus buy features are designed to provide players with more control over their gaming experience. They can be especially appealing to those who enjoy the bonus rounds more than the base game or who are looking to increase their chances of winning big.

How Do Bonus Buy Features Work?

When a player decides to use the bonus buy feature, they are essentially paying a premium to access the bonus round directly. The cost of this feature varies depending on the game and the specific bonus round being purchased. Typically, the cost is a multiple of the player's current bet size.

For example, if a player is betting $1 per spin and the bonus buy feature costs 100 times the bet, they would pay $100 to access the bonus round immediately. Once the fee is paid, the bonus round begins, and the player has the opportunity to win payouts based on the game's paytable.

It's important to note that while bonus buy features can increase the frequency of bonus rounds, they do not guarantee a win. The outcome of the bonus round is still determined by a random number generator (RNG), ensuring that each spin is independent and fair.
