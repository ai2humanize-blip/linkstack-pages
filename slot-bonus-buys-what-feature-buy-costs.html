What Are Slot Bonus Buys?

Slot bonus buys are a relatively new feature in online slot games that allow players to bypass the base game and instantly access the bonus round. This option is particularly appealing to players who prefer the excitement and potential rewards of bonus features over the standard spinning of reels. By purchasing a bonus round, players can immediately enjoy features such as free spins, multipliers, and other special gameplay elements that are often more lucrative than the base game.

The concept of buying bonuses has become increasingly popular due to its convenience and the potential for higher payouts. However, it is essential to understand the costs and mechanics involved before opting for this feature.

How Do Bonus Buys Work?

When a player chooses to buy a bonus, they pay a predetermined amount to access the bonus round immediately. The cost of this purchase varies depending on the game and the specific bonus feature being bought. Once the transaction is complete, the bonus round commences, and the player can enjoy the enhanced gameplay and potential rewards.

It is important to note that the outcome of the bonus round is still subject to the game's random number generator (RNG), meaning that the results are entirely random and not influenced by the fact that the bonus was bought.
