What is OxPolling?

OxPolling is an advanced voting protection system that leverages blockchain technology, encryption, and multi-factor authentication to safeguard the electoral process. The system is designed to prevent tampering, ensure voter anonymity, and provide a transparent audit trail. OxPolling is not a standalone solution but rather a suite of tools and protocols that can be integrated into existing voting systems.

How OxPolling Vote Protection Works

The OxPolling system employs several key mechanisms to protect votes and ensure the integrity of elections:

  • Blockchain Technology: At the core of OxPolling is blockchain technology, which provides a decentralized and immutable ledger. Each vote is recorded as a transaction on the blockchain, making it virtually impossible to alter or delete votes without detection. This ensures that the voting record remains tamper-proof and transparent.
  • Encryption: OxPolling uses advanced encryption techniques to protect voter data and votes. When a voter casts a ballot, the vote is encrypted before it is transmitted and stored. This ensures that even if data is intercepted, it cannot be read or altered without the proper decryption keys.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): To prevent unauthorized access, OxPolling incorporates multi-factor authentication. Voters are required to provide two or more verification factors, such as a password, a one-time code sent to their mobile device, or biometric data. This adds an extra layer of security, ensuring that only legitimate voters can cast their ballots.
  • Anonymous Voting: While ensuring security, OxPolling also protects voter anonymity. The system separates the identity of the voter from their vote, using cryptographic techniques to ensure that votes cannot be traced back to individuals. This maintains the privacy of voters while still allowing for the verification of election results.
  • Audit Trails: OxPolling provides a comprehensive audit trail that can be reviewed to verify the accuracy of election results. Each step of the voting process is logged, from voter registration to the casting of ballots, to the final tally. This allows for independent verification and transparency, which can help build public trust in the electoral process.
