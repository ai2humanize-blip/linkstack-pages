  • Authentication: To prevent fraudulent voting, Ox Polls implements rigorous authentication methods. These may include email verification, unique voting links, or even multi-factor authentication (MFA) for added security. This ensures that only eligible participants can cast their votes.
  • Audit Trails: The platform maintains detailed logs of all voting activities. This includes timestamps, IP addresses, and user actions. These audit trails provide a comprehensive record that can be reviewed to ensure the integrity of the poll and to detect any suspicious behavior.
  • Anonymity: Ox Polls ensures that votes are cast anonymously, protecting the privacy of participants. This is achieved through techniques such as anonymizing IP addresses and using randomized identifiers for each vote.
  • Data Integrity: The platform uses cryptographic techniques to ensure that once a vote is cast, it cannot be altered or deleted. This guarantees the accuracy of the poll results and prevents any form of manipulation.
  • Regular Security Audits: Ox Polls undergoes regular security audits and updates to address any potential vulnerabilities. This proactive approach ensures that the platform remains secure against emerging threats.
