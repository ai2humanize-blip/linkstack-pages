What is Oxpolling?

Oxpolling is a comprehensive system designed to enhance the security and reliability of elections. It integrates several key components, including:

  • Biometric Verification: This involves the use of unique biological identifiers such as fingerprints or facial recognition to verify the identity of voters. This ensures that only eligible individuals can cast their votes.
  • Blockchain Technology: By utilizing blockchain, oxpolling creates a decentralized and immutable ledger of votes. This means that once a vote is recorded, it cannot be altered or tampered with, providing a high level of security and transparency.
  • End-to-End Encryption: This ensures that all data transmitted during the voting process is encrypted, protecting it from unauthorized access or manipulation.
  • Audit Trails: Oxpolling systems maintain detailed logs of every step in the voting process, allowing for thorough audits and verification of results.

  • Authentication: On election day, voters arrive at the polling station and are authenticated using their biometric data. This can be done through fingerprint scanners or facial recognition technology. Once authenticated, the voter is granted access to the voting system.
  • Voting: The voter casts their vote using a secure electronic interface. The system ensures that each voter can only vote once and that their choices are recorded accurately. The use of blockchain technology ensures that the vote is stored in a tamper-proof manner.
  • Verification: After voting, the system provides a receipt or verification code that the voter can use to confirm that their vote has been recorded correctly. This step adds an additional layer of transparency and trust in the process.
  • Result Compilation: Once the voting period ends, the system compiles the results. The use of blockchain ensures that the results are accurate and cannot be altered. Detailed audit trails are available for review to ensure the integrity of the process.
