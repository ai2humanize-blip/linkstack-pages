The Legacy of Eloise

First published in 1955, 'Eloise' quickly became a cultural phenomenon. The books, featuring illustrations by Hilary Knight, follow the adventures of Eloise, a precocious and imaginative child who lives in the Plaza Hotel with her nanny, her pug, Weenie, and her turtle, Skipperdee. Eloise's charm lies in her boundless curiosity and her ability to turn everyday situations into grand adventures.

Over the years, Eloise has become a timeless character, capturing the hearts of children and adults alike. Her stories have been adapted into various formats, including television specials and animated series. Now, Netflix is adding to this legacy with a live-action movie that promises to bring a modern twist to the classic tales.
