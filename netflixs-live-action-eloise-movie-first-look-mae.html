Who is Eloise?

Eloise is a fictional character created by Kay Thompson in 1955, with illustrations by Hilary Knight. The series of children's books, set in New York's Plaza Hotel, follows the adventures of Eloise, a precocious and imaginative child who lives in the hotel with her nanny, her pet turtle, and her pug. Known for her iconic phrase "Eloise, age six," the character has become a symbol of youthful exuberance and curiosity.

The books have inspired various adaptations over the years, including animated specials and a Broadway musical. However, this will be the first time Eloise is portrayed in a live-action film, making it a highly anticipated event for both new and longtime fans.
