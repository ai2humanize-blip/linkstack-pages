Exploring Independent Websites: Guides, Reviews & How-Tos for 2023

Discover the best independent websites offering guides, reviews, and how-tos across various domains, including news, finance, and cryptocurrency. This roundup will help you navigate through a curated selection of websites that provide valuable insights, tools, and resources to stay informed and make better decisions.

More Independent Websites Worth Exploring

In an age where mainstream media often dominates the narrative, independent websites offer a refreshing perspective. These platforms are not bound by corporate interests, allowing them to deliver unbiased and diverse content. Here are some key reasons to explore independent websites:

  • Diverse Perspectives: Independent websites often provide a wide range of viewpoints, encouraging critical thinking and a broader understanding of complex issues.
  • Unbiased Content: Without the influence of large corporations or advertisers, these sites can focus on delivering honest and objective information.
  • Niche Topics: Many independent sites cater to specific interests, offering in-depth coverage on niche topics that mainstream media might overlook.

Whether you're interested in technology, culture, or politics, independent websites can enrich your knowledge and provide a more comprehensive view of the world.

A Reading List of Independent News and Finance Sites

Staying informed about current events and financial trends is crucial in today's fast-paced world. Independent news and finance sites offer a reliable alternative to mainstream media, providing in-depth analysis and investigative journalism. Here are some notable independent sites to consider:

  1. ProPublica: Known for its investigative journalism, ProPublica focuses on stories that hold power accountable and bring to light issues that affect the public.
  2. Reuters: Although it has a global presence, Reuters' independent journalism ensures that news is reported without bias, providing accurate and timely information.
  3. Seeking Alpha: This platform offers a wealth of financial analysis and insights, making it a valuable resource for investors looking to stay ahead of market trends.
  4. Jacobin: For those interested in political and economic analysis from a leftist perspective, Jacobin provides thought-provoking articles and commentary.

These sites, among others, offer a diverse range of news and financial analysis, helping you stay informed and make informed decisions.

Independent Crypto and Finance Publications to Bookmark

The world of cryptocurrency and finance is constantly evolving, and staying updated is essential for investors and enthusiasts alike. Independent crypto and finance publications provide in-depth analysis, market trends, and expert opinions. Here are some must-bookmark sites:

  • CoinDesk: A leading source for cryptocurrency news, CoinDesk offers comprehensive coverage of the latest developments in the crypto world, including market analysis and expert opinions.
  • Decrypt: Known for its user-friendly approach, Decrypt provides easy-to-understand articles and guides on cryptocurrency and blockchain technology.
  • Investopedia: While not exclusively focused on crypto, Investopedia offers a wealth of resources on finance and investment, including detailed guides and tutorials on cryptocurrency.
  • The Block: This site provides in-depth research and analysis on the blockchain and crypto industry, making it a valuable resource for those looking to understand the nuances of this rapidly changing field.

By bookmarking these sites, you can ensure that you have access to the latest news, analysis, and insights in the world of crypto and finance.

Useful Independent Sites, Tools, and Crypto Resources

In addition to news and analysis, independent websites offer a variety of tools and resources that can be incredibly useful for both beginners and seasoned professionals. Here are some examples:

  1. CryptoCompare: This site provides a comprehensive suite of tools for tracking cryptocurrency prices, market trends, and more. Its user-friendly interface makes it easy to stay on top of the latest developments.
  2. TradingView: Known for its advanced charting tools, TradingView is a favorite among traders for its real-time data and community-driven insights.
  3. CoinGecko: Offering a wide range of market data and analysis, CoinGecko is a go-to resource for those looking to track the performance of various cryptocurrencies.
  4. Messari: This site provides in-depth research and analysis on the crypto industry, offering a wealth of data and insights for those looking to understand the market better.

These tools and resources can help you make informed decisions, track your investments, and stay updated on the latest trends in the crypto world.

Key takeaways

Independent websites offer a wealth of information and resources across various domains, from news and finance to cryptocurrency. By exploring these sites, you can gain access to diverse perspectives, unbiased content, and in-depth analysis that can help you stay informed and make better decisions. Whether you're a casual reader or a seasoned investor, independent websites provide valuable insights and tools that are essential in today's fast-paced world.
