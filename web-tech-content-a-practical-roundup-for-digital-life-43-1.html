Web, Tech & Content: An Instagram Size Cheat-Sheet Designers Can Keep

The Instagram Size Cheat-Sheet Designers Can Keep is useful because social content is produced for several placements, each with a different shape. A single crop cannot serve every format, so planning exports by placement prevents clipped text, awkward reframing, and repeated redesign.

Build from the destination

A practical working sheet can start with these common dimensions, while designers should still confirm the latest platform requirements before a campaign:

  • Feed portrait: 4:5, commonly exported at 1080 × 1350 pixels.
  • Feed square: 1:1, commonly exported at 1080 × 1080 pixels.
  • Feed landscape: up to approximately 1.91:1, with 1080 × 566 pixels as a working export.
  • Reels and Stories: vertical 9:16, commonly exported at 1080 × 1920 pixels.
  • Profile image: a high-resolution square that remains legible when displayed as a small circle.

Keep the focal point near the center, especially in Stories and Reels, where profile details, captions, and other interface elements can cover the edges. Export files for their intended placement rather than uploading one master everywhere. If a project spans websites, advertisements, and social posts, create a small design system for shared colors, typography, logo clear space, and file naming. That turns the cheat sheet into a repeatable production standard rather than merely a dimension list.

A Practical Guide to Indonesian Government Documents: KTP, NPWP, BPJS and Passport

A Practical Guide to Indonesian Government Documents explains what four core documents are, why they matter, and when readers may need them. Knowing the issuing authority and purpose of each record makes it easier to prepare for applications involving identity, taxation, health coverage, or international travel.

What each document covers

  • KTP: Indonesia’s national identity card for citizens, containing registered identity and civil information.
  • NPWP: A taxpayer identification number used for taxation and related administrative processes.
  • BPJS Kesehatan: The card used for participation in the national health-insurance system. BPJS Ketenagakerjaan, or BPJS TK, is a separate employment-insurance program.
  • Passport: Evidence of nationality and identity for international travel, with entry and exit rules depending on the destination.

These documents may be requested for employment, banking, healthcare, tax administration, or travel, although each institution sets its own rules. The KTP, NPWP, BPJS Kesehatan card, and passport are not interchangeable: each answers a different administrative question. Keeping a secure personal document register—with record locations, issue details, and renewal reminders—can prevent an urgent application from being delayed by a missing card or expired credential.

Common Questions About Indonesian Documents and Services, Answered

Common Questions About Indonesian Documents and Services brings recurring uncertainties into one place. The useful starting point is to identify the responsible agency and confirm the current requirement before arranging travel, paying a fee, or uploading personal information.

Which office handles a request?

Civil registration and population records are generally handled by the relevant Dukcapil office; tax matters by a tax office; BPJS Kesehatan enrollment and cards by the health-insurance administration; and passport matters by immigration services. Local procedures, delegated services, and appointment systems can vary, so readers should verify the correct office for their location.

Can everything be completed online?

No. Some applications can begin or be paid online, but identity checks, biometrics, signature capture, original-document review, or first-time registration may require an in-person visit. Treat the online and counter stages as one process: save every reference number and confirmation before attending an appointment.

What should be done if a document is lost?

Report the loss to the issuing authority and follow its replacement process rather than using an unofficial workaround. The steps and supporting documents differ for identity cards, tax records, BPJS cards, and passports, and processing may also depend on whether the document was still valid when it was lost.

Will photocopies or foreign-language records work?

Only if the receiving office accepts them. Certified translations or legalization may be needed when a document is used in another country or by a private institution, but requirements depend on the receiving authority. Names, birth information, and identity numbers should also match across forms and supporting records.

How to Navigate Indonesian Public Services Online

How to Navigate Indonesian Public Services Online focuses on a controlled sequence rather than a single universal portal. Government services may be split across agencies, municipal systems, and dedicated applications, so a careful workflow reduces errors and protects personal data.

  1. Identify the agency. Match the request to civil registration, tax, health insurance, immigration, or another specific authority.
  2. Find the official channel. Use the agency’s verified website or application, checking the publisher and government identity carefully.
  3. Create a record correctly. Enter details exactly as they appear on the source document; mistakes can require correction at the counter.
  4. Prepare supporting files. Review the current document list, format, size, and certification rules before uploading.
  5. Submit and retain references. Save the application number, receipt, payment confirmation, appointment details, and every downloaded file.
  6. Track the status. Use the official account or reference system to check updates, respond to requests, and note any required visit.
  7. Verify the result. Confirm that digital records match the issued document before relying on it for another application.

Readers should be cautious with third-party agents who promise guaranteed appointments or faster approval. Passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, and payment details should remain on the official channel. Search results can include paid advertisements that imitate public services, so the domain, agency branding, and contact information deserve a second look. When rules are unclear, contacting the issuing office before submitting is usually more efficient than completing the wrong form or travelling with incomplete paperwork.

Key takeaways

Good digital work begins with format-specific exports, while reliable document handling begins with knowing which agency owns each task. For Indonesian public services, verify requirements, protect personal information, and keep every reference until the process is complete.
