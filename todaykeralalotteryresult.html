Overview of Kerala State Lottery

The Kerala State Lottery was established in 1967, making it one of the oldest lottery systems in India. It is known for its transparency and fairness, ensuring that every participant has an equal chance of winning. The lottery is governed by the Department of Taxes, Government of Kerala, and offers a variety of schemes to cater to different preferences.

Some of the popular lottery schemes under the Kerala State Lottery include:

  • Win-Win: Draw held on Mondays
  • Sthree Sakthi: Draw held on Tuesdays
  • Akshaya: Draw held on Wednesdays
  • Karunya Plus: Draw held on Thursdays
  • Nirmal: Draw held on Fridays
  • Kerala Karunya: Draw held on Saturdays
  • Pournami: Draw held on Sundays
